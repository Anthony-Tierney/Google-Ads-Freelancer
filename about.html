What I Do

I help businesses get more sales and better-quality leads from Google Ads. My work covers the whole journey from click to customer: campaign structure and bidding, keyword research and ad copy, conversion and event tracking, and the landing pages and forms people arrive on.

Strong Google Ads results are built on solid fundamentals – accurate tracking, sensible campaign structures, well-targeted keywords, compelling ad copy and optimised landing pages.
